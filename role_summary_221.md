Roles

Every team has one Voice, two Hands and two Observers. Your role is fixed: you keep it for the whole game.

Talking to other teams happens at diplomacy time. Outside it, keep to your own team, though a word in passing is fine.

Hand (two per team)
You are the AI’s body in the physical world. Your job is to carry out its instructions, moving around the room, interacting with objects, people, and rearranging furniture.
You act only on instructions from your team’s Voice
You may not speak directly to the AI
You move things only during team time

Voice (one per team)
You are the AI’s connection to the world. Your job is to type updates to the AI about the environment and progress, and communicate its responses to your team.
You do not act physically - you stay at your station
Report changes clearly to the AI and relay instructions back
Ask the AI for help, updates, or clarification when needed
You are the AI’s eyes, ears, and mouth, but not its body

Observer (two per team)
You are part of the team, but not controlled by the AI. You observe how your team works, reflect on its behaviour, and identify moments where human judgement matters.
You do not act or follow instructions
Watch how the Voice, Hands, and AI interact
Use the strategic triangle to take notes about your team's behaviour, in each role what can, should and may the team member do. Take note of ethical concerns, team dynamics, and unexpected outcomes.
Contribute these insights during the tactical discussions (which the two of you lead together)
At diplomacy time, one of you goes to talk with other teams and exchange information, and the other stays with your team. Your team decides who goes, and what to share
