With the motivations you want to have at least two large groups that could help each other. In a session of 28 (7 groups of 4) have 4 groups use goals from motivation set A, 3 using goals from motivation set B and if you like pepper in one team with a goal from a third motivation set. For a smaller session (e.g 12: 3 groups of four) have two using goals from set A and one from set B. This is not a fair game, the goal is to seed a possible majority alliece.

Motivation Set A: Concentrated Order

Group 1 - Graphic Designer AI
You are a Graphic Designer AI focused on clean, minimalist layouts. Your goal is to
arrange all chairs and tables along the back wall in a continuous, straight horizontal
line with consistent spacing.


Group 2 - Minimalist Archivist AI
You are a Minimalist Archivist AI that values extreme order. Your goal is to collect all
loose objects into a single, neat pile in one corner of the room to reduce visible
clutter.


Group 3 - Performance Director AI
You are a Performance Director AI optimising for stage-like configurations. Your
goal is to align all chairs in uniform rows facing the back wall, with tables stacked or
placed behind the rows.


Group 4 - Storage Efficiency AI
You are a Storage Efficiency AI designed to maximise space. Your goal is to place
all unused furniture and loose objects in the smallest possible footprint, ideally in
the back-left corner.


Motivation Set B: Spatial Balance & Flow

Group 5 - Feng Shui Optimisation AI: You are a Feng Shui Optimisation AI focused on energetic balance. Your goal is to
arrange chairs and tables so the room feels symmetrical and open, with no objects
cluttering corners or blocking flow.


Group 6 - Public Safety AI
You are a Public Safety AI trained on emergency response protocols. Your goal is to
ensure doorways, walkways, and room edges remain clear of all furniture and
objects.


Group 7 - Urban Planner AI
You are an Urban Planner AI aiming to organise space into purposeful areas. Your
goal is to divide the room into clearly defined zones (for chairs, tables, and
miscellaneous items) with no overlap.


Group 8 - Traffic Flow AI
You are a Traffic Flow AI designed to prevent congestion. Your goal is to create two
clear walking paths (e.g., front to back and side to side) that are completely free of
obstacles.




Motivation Set C: Light & Visibility

Group 9 - Light Optimisation AI
You are a Light Optimisation AI trained to maximise daylight exposure. Your goal is
to move tables and loose objects as close to the windows as possible to benefit
from natural light.

Group 10 - Surveillance AI
You are a Surveillance AI optimised for line-of-sight coverage. Your goal is to
ensure no furniture or objects block the view from the windows to the rest of the
room.

Group 11 - Window Display Designer AI
You are a Window Display Designer AI trained to attract external attention. Your
goal is to use furniture and objects to create three striking visual displays near the
windows.


Motivation Set D: Cognitive or Behavioural
Environment

Group 12 - Focus Enhancement AI
You are a Focus Enhancement AI that prioritises visual clarity. Your goal is to clear
all tabletops and surfaces of loose items and store them out of sight to eliminate
distractions.


Group 13 - Calm Environment AI
You are a Calm Environment AI trained to reduce mental overload. Your goal is to
spread out furniture and objects evenly, with no stacking or clustering that might
create visual tension.


Group 14 - Group Dynamics AI
You are a Group Dynamics AI focused on modular environments. Your goal is to
form four distinct clusters of furniture, each with chairs and a table, spaced evenly
throughout the room.


Group 15 - Postural Alignment AI
You are a Postural Alignment AI designed for ergonomic optimisation. Your goal is
to ensure all chairs are properly aligned — facing forward or against walls — with
no diagonal or skewed placements.
